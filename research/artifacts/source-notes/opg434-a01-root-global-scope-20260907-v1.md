## Exact implication for this continuation

If that theorem is imported with its proof obligations satisfied, a non-B-
mappable subcubic graph cannot have girth at least seventeen. Combining it
with C12's candidate square exclusion would give ordinary girth in [5,16].
This does not imply girth five or the existence of a pentagon. The current
24-vertex girth-seven positive graph explicitly rejects an implication from
the stated small-cut/connectivity/girth-lower-bound properties to a pentagon.
It does NOT reject an implication whose premise includes minimum non-B-
mappability, because it has an explicit B-map. The missing use of this
extra premise is preserved as the first open structural obligation.
